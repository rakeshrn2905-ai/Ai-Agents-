## Title
Your Keyboard Is 150 Years Old

## Description
Your keyboard layout is literally Victorian. Here's where QWERTY came from - and why we never switched.

QWERTY comes from the Sholes & Glidden typewriter, sold by Remington in 1874. Christopher Sholes and his team kept moving the keys around for years. Was it to stop the type bars jamming? Historians still argue - one study links it to telegraph operators instead. In 1893 the biggest typewriter makers merged, and QWERTY became the standard. Your phone can't jam, but we never switched.
