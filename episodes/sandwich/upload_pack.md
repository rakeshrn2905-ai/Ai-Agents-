## Title
The Sandwich Is Named After a Real Earl (1762)

## Description
Your lunch is named after a real person: John Montagu, the 4th Earl of Sandwich. But the famous story about him probably isn't true.

Legend says he ate meat between bread so he could keep gambling. That story comes from a 1770 travel book that doesn't even name him - his biographer thinks he ate that way while working at his desk at the Admiralty. He didn't invent the idea, but the name 'sandwich' was already in use by 1762. And Captain Cook named Hawaii the 'Sandwich Islands' after him.
