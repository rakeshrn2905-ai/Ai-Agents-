## Title
France Used the Guillotine Until 1977

## Description
France was still using the guillotine in 1977 - the same year Star Wars came out.

The guillotine was first used in France in April 1792, during the Revolution. The last public execution was in 1939; after that, executions happened behind prison walls. The very last one took place in Marseille on 10 September 1977. France abolished the death penalty in 1981. Disco, cassettes - and the guillotine. Same year.
