## Title
You Didn't Want a Tumble Dryer. So You Built a Tower.

## Description
Drying clothes indoors makes the whole house damp, and a tumble dryer costs money every time you run it. So you put up a washing line.

The first part is how a rotary washing line really goes in: dig a hole in the lawn, drop in a ground socket, fill round it with quick-setting concrete and hold it level while it sets. Then the line slots in.

Everything after the rain is a joke. Please do not build a wind tower in your garden.
