## Title
Bluetooth Was Named After a Viking King (1990s)

## Description
Your Bluetooth is named after a Viking.

Harald "Bluetooth" Gormsson was a 10th-century king of Denmark, famous for uniting Denmark and Norway. In the 1990s, Intel engineer Jim Kardach was reading about him while working on a short-range wireless link meant to unite phones and computers - so he borrowed the king's name as a code name, and it stuck. The logo is a bind rune: Harald's initials, H (Hagall) and B (Bjarkan), in Viking runes, merged into one symbol.
