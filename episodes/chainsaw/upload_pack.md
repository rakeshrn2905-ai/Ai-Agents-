## Title
Chainsaws Were Invented for Childbirth (1780s)

## Description
The chainsaw was invented for childbirth.

In the 1780s, two Scottish doctors, John Aitken and James Jeffray, are credited with the first chain saw: a fine chain with tiny serrated teeth, pulled back and forth by two handles. It was made for difficult births, where bone sometimes had to be cut and a knife was painfully slow. Later versions got a hand crank. Over a century later, in 1905, San Francisco logger Samuel Bens patented an "endless chain saw" meant for giant redwoods - and the tool moved from the doctor's bag to the forest.
