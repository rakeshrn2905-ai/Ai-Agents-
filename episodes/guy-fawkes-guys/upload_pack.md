## Title
'Guys' Comes From a 1605 Gunpowder Plot

## Description
Every time you say "guys", you're quoting Bonfire Night. Here's how Guy Fawkes went from gunpowder plotter to the word for everyone.

In 1605 Guy Fawkes guarded 36 barrels of gunpowder under the House of Lords. A warning letter exposed the plot, England lit bonfires to celebrate the king's survival, and later people burned straw dummies of Fawkes, called "guys". By the 1830s a guy meant an oddly dressed person, by 1847 any man - and now, all of you guys.
