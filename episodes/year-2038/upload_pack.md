## Title
Some Clocks Will Hit 1901 in 2038

## Description
On 19 January 2038, some computers will think it's 1901. It's called the Year 2038 problem - here's why it happens and how it's being fixed.

Many computers count time as seconds since 1 January 1970. Older systems store that count in a 32-bit number, which tops out at 2,147,483,647 - reached at 03:14:07 UTC on 19 January 2038. One second later the number flips negative and those clocks read 13 December 1901. The fix is 64-bit time, which lasts about 292 billion years. Most modern devices already use it; older and embedded systems are the ones at risk.
