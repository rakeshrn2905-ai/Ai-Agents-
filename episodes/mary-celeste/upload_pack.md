## Title
The 1872 Ghost Ship Found With Nobody Aboard

## Description
In December 1872, sailors found the Mary Celeste drifting in the Atlantic - with nobody on board.

Her lifeboat was missing, but about six months of food and water were still there, and her cargo of 1,701 barrels of industrial alcohol was mostly untouched. Captain Benjamin Briggs, his wife, their two-year-old daughter and seven crew were never seen again. Theories range from alcohol fumes to a storm to mutiny, but none has ever been proven. (The "half-eaten breakfast" story is a myth - it comes from a short story Arthur Conan Doyle wrote in 1884.)
