## Title
The WW2 Army That Fought With Balloons

## Description
In World War Two, the US Army built a unit of artists that fought with inflatable rubber tanks, loudspeakers and fake radio chatter.

The 23rd Headquarters Special Troops - the "Ghost Army" - was about 1,100 men, many recruited from art schools (fashion designer Bill Blass and painter Ellsworth Kelly among them). They set up inflatable tanks, jeeps and artillery, played recorded sounds of troops and vehicles, and sent fake radio traffic, all to trick the enemy about where the real Allied units were. The whole thing stayed top secret until 1996. In 2024 the unit was awarded the Congressional Gold Medal.
