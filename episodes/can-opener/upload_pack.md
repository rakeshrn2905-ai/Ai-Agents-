## Title
Cans Came 40 Years Before Can Openers

## Description
For about 40 years, opening a can meant a hammer and a chisel. Here's why the tin can had to wait so long for its opener.

Peter Durand patented the tin can in 1810. Early cans were thick iron lined with tin - some weighed more than the food inside - so you hacked them open with a hammer and chisel. By the 1850s cans were thinner, and the first can-opener patents finally appeared (England 1850s, USA 1858).
