## Title
Why Outlook Went Down Worldwide (12 Nov)

## Description
As of 14 November 2026: on 12 November, Microsoft's Outlook email and Teams stopped working for hours for users around the world.

Why? According to Microsoft's status updates, some Exchange Online email servers failed their automated health checks and dropped out of service. Their work shifted to the servers that were still running, until those became overloaded too - a domino effect. Microsoft said there was no sign of malicious activity and that traffic had not increased. Microsoft apologised, said service was restored, and has promised a full post-incident report, so the final cause may be described in more detail later.

And email itself? It is 55 years old: engineer Ray Tomlinson sent the first network email in 1971.
