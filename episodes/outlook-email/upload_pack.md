## Title
Outlook Went Down. Email Is 55 Years Old.

## Description
As of 14 November 2026: on 12 November, Microsoft's Outlook and other Microsoft 365 services went down for users around the world. Microsoft apologised and later said service was restored.

But email itself is 55 years old. In 1971, engineer Ray Tomlinson sent the first network email between two computers standing side by side, linked only through ARPANET, the network that came before the internet. He needed a way to separate the person's name from the machine, so he picked the @ sign. And the first message? He later said the test messages were so forgettable that he forgot them.
