## Title
Cleopatra Was Closer to 1969 Than the Pyramids

## Description
Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid. Here's the maths.

The Great Pyramid of Giza was finished around 2560 BC. Cleopatra died in 30 BC - about 2,500 years later. The Moon landing was in 1969, only about 2,000 years after her. To Cleopatra, the pyramids were already ancient.
