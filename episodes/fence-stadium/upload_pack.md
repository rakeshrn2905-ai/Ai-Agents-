## Description
You only wanted a garden fence.

The first part is how a fence really goes in: a post hole about 600 mm deep, a little gravel so water drains away, fast-setting concrete round the post, a gravel board to keep the timber off the wet ground, then a 1.83 m panel. In England a fence can usually be up to 2 m high without planning permission, as long as it is not next to a road (listed buildings and some conservation areas differ).
