## Title
Treadmills Were Built to Punish Prisoners (1818)

## Description
The treadmill started as a prison punishment.

In 1818, British engineer William Cubitt saw prisoners sitting idle and designed the prison treadwheel: a giant wooden wheel of steps that prisoners kept climbing, holding a rail, for hours at a time. Many wheels ground grain or pumped water - but some were connected to nothing at all, pure punishment. Britain stopped using them around 1900 (the Prison Act of 1898 ended them; the last went by 1902). Today we pay a gym to do the same thing.
