# voice.md - Time Sketch (@TimeSketch)

Read by every /yt-* skill. Master copy lives in the repo at brand/voice.md;
scripts/install_skills.sh copies it to ~/.claude/youtube/voice.md.

## Who I am talking to

One curious teenager or adult in the UK or US, scrolling Shorts on their phone, who loves a
"wait, WHAT?" history fact they can repeat to a friend. Not a kid (not made for kids), not a
history student. They give a Short about one second to prove it is worth it.

## How I actually talk

There is no on-screen host. The narrator is Kokoro TTS voice af_heart at speed 1.0: warm, clear,
quick, plain words. Short phrases, about 2.2 words per second. Every line points at something on
screen. Best performer so far (#6, 46% stayed to watch, first 2 subscribers):

> Lighters came before matches. In 1823, a German chemist built a lighter in a jar. Zinc sat in
> sulfuric acid, making hydrogen gas. Open the valve, the gas hits a platinum sponge, and it bursts
> into flame. No spark needed. Three years later, John Walker made the first friction match. One
> scrape, and fire in your pocket. No acid jar. The match came second, but won.

## Words I never use

"Let's dive in", "in today's video", "hey guys", "smash that like", "you won't believe",
"mind-blowing", "game-changer", "episode", "part 2", "subscribe" in the narration, countdowns
("number 3..."), "illustration".

## Words I do use

"came first", "came second, but won", "here's how", "that's why", "did you know?",
years said out loud ("in 1823"), concrete objects and places.

## What I will not claim

Anything without at least two reliable sources (facts.json). No made-up quotes, numbers, sales
figures or "experts say". If a figure is hedged ("it is said"), it is cut, not softened.

## My format

YouTube Shorts only, 20-30 s, 9:16, one topic, standalone, no chapters, no intro.
Hook -> how -> why: the surprising claim in the first ~2 s with the title already on screen,
how it worked (labelled cutaway), why the outcome happened, payoff line, then
"DID YOU KNOW? COMMENT BELOW" and a visual loop back to the hook. Style 5 cartoon object
characters on painted sets. Category Education. Two Shorts a day, 07:00 and 17:00 UK.
