# Pre-pick fact check (8 Nov 2026) - T14, T15, T6, T1

## T14 Pluto - SAFE
- Discovered 18 Feb 1930 (Clyde Tombaugh, Lowell Observatory); orbit ~248 years; first full orbit since discovery ends ~23 March 2178.
  Sources: livescience.com/33390-pluto-first-complete-orbit.html, iflscience.com (2178), earth.com, wikipedia Pluto.
- Avoid: the exact day (a calculation, not an event) - say "in 2178".

## T15 First webcam - SAFE, with one date nuance
- 1991: Cambridge Computer Lab researchers (Quentin Stafford-Fraser, Paul Jardetzky) pointed a camera at the Trojan Room coffee pot to avoid trips to an empty pot.
- Nov 1993: put on the web; switched off 22 Aug 2001 (lab moved).
  Sources: guinnessworldrecords.com (first webcam), historyofinformation.com/detail.php?id=1507, theregister.co.uk 2001/03/07.
- Avoid: "the first livestream on the internet" in 1991 (it was a local network at first); the Der Spiegel auction price (single source).

## T6 Vending machine - SAFE, keep mechanism general
- Hero of Alexandria described a coin-operated holy-water dispenser in the Pneumatica (1st century AD, dates uncertain).
- A coin falls on a pan on a lever, tilts it, opens a valve, water flows; the coin slides off and the valve shuts.
  Sources: mentalfloss.com (first vending machine), todayifoundout.com (Hero), hackaday.com 2021/03/12, historyofinformation.com.
- Avoid: "five drachma" price and "commissioned by a temple" (single/unverified); say "about 2,000 years ago".

## T1 Viking helmets - SAFE
- No evidence Vikings wore horned helmets; the image spread from Carl Emil Doepler's costumes for Wagner's Ring cycle (Bayreuth, 1876), plus 1800s artists.
- Horned helmets did exist much earlier (Bronze Age, e.g. Veksø helmets) - not Viking.
  Sources: history.com (did Vikings wear horned helmets), mentalfloss.com, OUP blog (ten myths), wikipedia Horned helmet.
- Avoid: "Doepler copied Native American headdresses" (single doubtful source).
